● Diagnosis: Energy Decoupling Between Columns and Classification

  The 6.68% test accuracy (worse than 10% random chance) results from a fundamental architectural mismatch between the predictive coding energy landscape and the classification objective.

  Root Cause: Gaussian Energy Dominance

  Looking at the graph structure:

  ResNet stages → stage taps (4×) → columns (4×) → combiner → pool → classifier
                [GaussianEnergy]   [GaussianEnergy] [GaussianEnergy]  [CrossEntropyEnergy]

  The problem mechanism:

  1. Each intermediate node minimizes its own Gaussian energy: E = Σ (z_latent - z_mu)²
    - Stage taps, columns, and combiner all use GaussianEnergy (the default)
    - The classifier is the only node using CrossEntropyEnergy
  2. Energy ratio overwhelms classification signal: With 4 stage taps + 4 columns + 1 combiner = 9 nodes using Gaussian energy vs. 1 classifier using CrossEntropy, the total energy landscape is dominated by Gaussian terms.
  3. Columns minimize energy without learning discriminative features: A column can minimize its Gaussian energy by producing z_mu that matches its randomly initialized z_latent (std=0.02, near zero). This is achieved by driving outputs toward zero, regardless of whether those outputs help classification.
  4. Why accuracy declines as energy decreases:
    - Epoch 1-5: Energy unstable (0.2 → 7.0), columns haven't converged to trivial solution yet
    - Epoch 5: Peak accuracy 10.4% (random-ish features still provide some signal)
    - Epoch 6-10: Energy drops to 0.08, columns converge to near-zero outputs → combiner receives weak signal → classifier starves → accuracy drops to 6.2%

  Evidence from the Code

  In depth_spanning_column.py:424-435:
  # Compute error
  error = state.z_latent - z_mu

  # Compute energy (GaussianEnergy by default)
  state = node_class.energy_functional(state, node_info)

  The column's energy is (z_latent - z_mu)². Since z_latent is initialized near zero (NormalInitializer std=0.02), the columns learn to produce near-zero z_mu, minimizing their local energy but destroying the classification signal.
